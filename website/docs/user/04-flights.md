# Flights

**Flights** is the logbook: every flight, one by one. Use it to look up a flight, or to organise many at once.

## Browse flights

As on [Sessions](03-sessions.md), choose **Cards** or **Table** at the top right, and narrow the list with **Filters**.

Select a flight to open it: its pilot, times, air time, distance, the drone, controller and battery it used, and its track on the map.

## Organise flights

In the **Table** view, select flights with the checkboxes, then choose **New session**, **Add to session**, or an action from the menu beside them, such as **Set pilot**.

Flights that didn't join a session by themselves are found with the filter **Session: Without session**. The **Suggestions** view then groups them into sessions for you to accept.

## Archive a flight

Archive a flight that shouldn't count, such as a test hover: open it and select **Archive flight**. To bring it back, filter on **Status: Archived** and select **Restore**.
