# Areas

Areas are named pieces of land, such as "North block" or "River section", that you draw on a map. FleetRanger then shows how long each flight and session spent in each area, and you can filter flights and sessions by the areas they flew over.

Areas are off for a new organisation. An admin switches them on under [Administration](08-administration.md), with **Edit settings** and **Areas**. Only then does **Areas** appear in the menu.

## Draw an area

The **Areas** page lists the areas beside a map of them.

1. Select **New area**, in the list or among the map's buttons under the zoom buttons.
2. Click the map for each corner. Close the shape by clicking the first corner again.
3. Enter a **Name** and select **Save**.

While drawing, corners snap to the corners and edges of other areas, so neighbouring areas can share a border. Select **Cancel** to stop.

Areas don't overlap. Where a new area overlaps others, it is cut off at their border; the other areas stay as they are. If cutting splits the drawing into pieces, each piece becomes an area with the same name.

Drawing and editing work on a computer. On a phone, the page only shows the areas.

## Change or delete an area

Select an area on the map or in the list. The menu on its row has:

- **Edit shape**: drag the corners, then select **Save**.
- **Rename**.
- **Delete**. This can't be undone.

## How time per area is counted

A flight's areas come from its track, not from what the pilot reports. Flights and sessions show the air time in each area, in minutes. Areas with the same name count as one.

Changing or deleting an area recounts every flight, also past ones, so earlier figures change with it.

Switching areas off hides the page, the times per area and the areas on maps. The areas themselves are kept, and come back when you switch them on again.

## Who may do what

- Only an **Admin** sees the **Areas** page and draws, changes and deletes areas.
- Everyone sees the time per area of the flights and sessions they can see.
